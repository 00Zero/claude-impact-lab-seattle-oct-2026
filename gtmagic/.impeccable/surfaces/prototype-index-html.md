# Surface: GTMagic chat (prototype/index.html)

Mode: Operate. Static HTML/CSS/JS skeleton of the chat-led run; code-led build.

Audience and job: a small-business owner or founder (or a judge on venue Wi-Fi, often on a phone) describes their business in a chat, or taps Try an example, then watches research, the synthetic crowd, and the results arrive in the thread, and asks follow-up questions.

Constraints: plain English (no "segment", "ICP", "lexicon", "stability"); counts and n beside every simulated number; real quotes vs simulated words visibly different; drafts labeled; example run badge; privacy note at input; loading, empty, error states; works at 390px.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One figure is one customer. The answer is a picture-statistic counted by eye, not a bar chart or a percentage. Refuses the category-default chat wrapper (grey sidebar, blue accent, neutral cards with progress bars).

OWN-WORLD: Isotype picture statistics. Near-white ground #fbfbf8, ink #1d1d1b rail, four flat group colours (red #c8372a, blue #2c5aa0, ochre #d99a1e, green #3d7a4f), none-of-these as grey outline figures. Jost (Futura lineage, as Isotype's own type) in two weights; flat colour, no gradients, no rounded-card shadows; plates ruled by a 3px ink line.

STORY: The owner tells GTMagic about the business, sees real quotes with sources, watches 48 figures sort themselves into rows, then reads three plain answers, the honest guess verdict, and a test plan for this week.
