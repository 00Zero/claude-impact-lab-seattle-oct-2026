Step 2 · The pipeline (click a step)

From raw media to a finished reel

Pre-tagging & de-duplication: real results

141 photos → 95 unique scenes

Quality rating per photo

hero = best for ads · exclude = never post

Automatically excluded

  • 4 mirror selfies of the photographer
  • 2 blurry frames
  • 1 shot with the photographer reflected in a black cabinet
  • House number visible → swapped for a clean angle, or blurred
  • "ROAD WORK" sign in the front shot → auto-cropped

Drone videos: keyframes only

4 frames per clip (10/35/65/90%) instead of decoding 7.3 GB. That gives each clip its stage, subject and best moments.

v02: facade → fly through the window into the framed interior. Tagged as the best "hook" shot.

Correction: drag the slider

Verticals made vertical

beforeafter

Window wall: verticals converged 6.4° (camera pointed up) → fixed

beforeafter

Rear exterior: camera rolled 3.9° → leveled

How

  • Detect wall, door and window edges
  • Fit tilt (camera roll) and keystone (camera pitch)
  • One perspective warp, then crop the empty corners
  • Guardrails: skip photos with too few lines and anything over 7° (could be intentional)

Then every still gets motion: eased push-ins, pans and pull-outs, rendered from 2× frames so they don't jitter.

Step 3 · Pick a template

4 viral templates, filled with your best media

What the research found works: a concrete number in the first 3 s · 15–30 s · text on screen the whole time (most viewers watch muted) · a walk-through order that feels like being there · one word to comment as the CTA.

Agent 2 · The CRM front desk

From "Comment BUILD" to a booked call

Viewer comments BUILD
→

→

1–2 questions at a time
→
Offers 3 real calendar slots
→
Books + Meet link
→
📲 SMS: "Jane, custom home, Kirkland, has land, 6–12 mo, Tue 2pm"

Collects

Project type · location · land or house · size · budget range · timeline · financing · plans/permits · the reel they liked · contact info

Guardrails

  • Never quotes prices or estimates, enforced by a regex check after every reply
  • Hands off to a human on complaints, legal talk, or "real person"
  • Only offers slots that actually exist on the calendar
  • Ignores duplicate webhooks and its own messages

Human in the loop

The team gets the full lead summary by SMS and the calendar invite. They walk into the call already knowing the project.
