Keep your whole shop on the same page. Just say it.

## Purpose

New owners of cafés, bakeries, salons and pop-up shops make dozens of decisions a day about prices, hours, menus and refunds, usually with nothing written down. Those decisions get lost, contradict each other, or never reach the menu, website, register or staff. Clark logs each spoken change, flags conflicts with past decisions, and writes a checklist of what to update plus a staff message.

## Try it

- Live app: https://tryclark.live (backup: https://clark-fg99.onrender.com)
- Demo video: https://youtube.com/shorts/wbdUouXvQxM

1. Open the link and tap "Try it with a sample café"
2. Tap the mic and say a change, or type it
3. Tap "Send to Clark"

Note: the free server can take up to a minute to wake up. Chrome works best for voice; typing works everywhere.

## What's still rough
