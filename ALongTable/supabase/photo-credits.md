# Photo credits for the Seattle demo data

`scripts/seed-seattle-demo.mjs` creates ten **invented** Seattle restaurants with sample spaces, events and guests. The restaurants,
events and people are made up, and **the photos are not photos of these places**: each is a freely reusable image from
Wikimedia Commons, released into the public domain or under CC0, so no attribution is required. They are listed here so the
source of every image is on record. Replace them with real photos (a restaurant can upload its own from the dashboard) when you have them.
