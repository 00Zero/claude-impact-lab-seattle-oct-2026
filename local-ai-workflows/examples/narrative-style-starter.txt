Client Discussed:

Do not assert unsupported diagnoses or interpretations. Include up to three short exact client quotes when available; do not invent quotes to meet a quota.

Therapist Contribution:

Next Actions:
Include this section only if a next action or topic to revisit was explicitly stated. Otherwise omit this heading and section entirely. Do not invent goals, homework, recommendations, or future plans.
