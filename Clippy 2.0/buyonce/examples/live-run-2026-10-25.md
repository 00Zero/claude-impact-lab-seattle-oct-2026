## What the run taught us

- The skill's draft steps matched the live site. No step needed rewriting.
- **One slip:** a click by screen coordinate missed because the page had scrolled and landed on a product description. Nothing was added. Clicking by element reference fixed it. Lesson written into the skill's quirks.
- The controller did its job: it refused to hand out cart steps until approval, handed sign-in to the owner, verified the cart item and the Overview total against the approval, and released the final step only on the sandbox host.

## What this does not show

- A real vendor. On a real site the controller stops before the transaction step and hands over to the owner.
- Prices on a real site, shipping choices, or stock handling.
- Several vendors compared from live quotes (only one vendor was quoted).
