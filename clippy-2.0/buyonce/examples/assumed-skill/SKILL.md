# Reorder from Harvest Wholesale

Recorded 2026-10-20 from the owner's screen and voice. This is a stand-in for what the capture teammate's recorder produces: numbered steps, loose wording, no fixed section names. BuyOnce's executor must cope with this shape.

The owner buys flour and other bakery staples here about twice a month. They are already signed in on their computer.

## What the owner does

1. Open https://harvestwholesale.example in the browser (already signed in).
2. Click "Quick Order" in the top menu.
3. Type the product name {item} in the search box and press Enter.
4. Pick the 50 lb bag listing. The owner says: never the 25 lb bag, the price per pound is worse.
5. Set the quantity to {qty}.
6. Check the price under the quantity box, the shipping line, and the delivery date.
7. Click "Add to cart".
8. Open the cart. Make sure shipping says "Standard Freight", not "Express".
9. Click "Checkout".
10. Choose the saved address "Main Bakery".
11. Review the order and click "Place order".
12. The saved card on file is charged. The owner screenshots the confirmation page.

## Owner's notes while recording
- "I look at delivery date before anything else. Friday deliveries are no good for me."
- "If the shipping line is more than twenty dollars I call them instead."
