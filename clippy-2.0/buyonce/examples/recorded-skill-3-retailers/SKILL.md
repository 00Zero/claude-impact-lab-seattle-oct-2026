# Buy supplies: check Amazon, Walmart, Target, then order

Recorded 2026-10-25 from the owner's screen. Stand-in for the capture teammate's output: numbered steps, loose wording. The owner is already signed in to all three sites in Chrome.

## What the owner does

1. Open https://www.amazon.com.
2. Type {item} in the search box and press Enter.
3. Open the first result that is the right size and sold by Amazon or a seller with good reviews. The owner says: skip anything "sponsored", skip sellers under 4 stars.
4. Write down the price, the shipping line, and the delivery date.
5. Open https://www.walmart.com in a new tab.
6. Type {item} in the search box and press Enter.
7. Open the matching listing. Write down the price, shipping, and delivery date. The owner says: only "sold and shipped by Walmart", not marketplace sellers.
8. Open https://www.target.com in a new tab.
9. Type {item} in the search box and press Enter.
10. Open the matching listing. Write down the price, shipping, and delivery date.
11. Compare the three. The owner picks the lowest total (price plus shipping) that arrives by {need_by}. If two are within a dollar, take the one they have ordered from before.
12. Go to the winner's tab and click "Add to cart".
13. Open the cart and check the item and quantity {qty}.
14. Click "Checkout" and check the shipping address is the shop's address.
15. Pay with the card saved on the account.
16. Click "Place order".
17. Screenshot the confirmation page and email it to the bookkeeper.
