### Booking app double books (25, 10%, 2.1★)

Since the spring app update, customers report double bookings, appointments cancelled or moved on their own, and even no-show fees charged for app-cancelled visits.

**Try:** Roll back or fix the app update, refund every no-show fee charged on an app-cancelled booking, and offer phone booking until it is stable.

> Hello, just letting you know the app is charging the no-show fee for appointments it cancelled itself. I was charged $25 for a groom that the app cancelled on me. Please refund.
>

> Booking used to be painless. Since around April the app has been double booking and randomly cancelling. Feels like it was rushed out.
>

### Pickups run hours late (15, 6%, 2.1★)

**Try:** Quote pickup windows based on the actual day's schedule, and text owners as soon as a delay becomes clear rather than at the end.

## How this was made

Everything except naming the groups ran on this computer: the text was turned into vectors with all-MiniLM-L6-v2, laid out with UMAP, grouped with HDBSCAN and drawn with DataMapPlot (UMAP, HDBSCAN and DataMapPlot are open source from the Tutte Institute for Mathematics and Computing).
